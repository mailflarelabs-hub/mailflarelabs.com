Common questions

The alert didn't appear for a message.
Check that a rule matches the sender or subject (rules are case-insensitive; domains also match subdomains), that alerts aren't paused, and that quiet hours aren't active. Messages that arrived before MailFlare was launched never alert, by design.
Can it alert on every email?
No — a rule with no senders, domains, or keywords never matches. MailFlare is meant for the few messages you can't miss.
Does it read my email?
Only the sender, subject, and preview snippet of new unread messages, locally, to check your rules. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I disconnect my Google account?
Settings → Gmail Account → Sign Out. MailFlare revokes its access with Google and removes the token from your Keychain.
