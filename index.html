MailFlare

A macOS menu bar app that shows an unmissable full-screen alert when an important email arrives in Gmail.

Privacy PolicySupport

What it does

You tell MailFlare which senders, domains, or subject keywords matter — your manager, your on-call paging service, a customer escalation list. When a matching message lands in your Gmail inbox, MailFlare draws a full-screen alert on every display showing who it's from, the subject, and a short preview, and keeps it there until you dismiss it or open the message.

Because the alert is a real app window rather than a notification, Focus modes, Do Not Disturb, and notification settings don't suppress it. That's the point: for the handful of messages you genuinely must not miss, "I had notifications off" stops being a reason.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account. MailFlare requests read-only access to Gmail — it cannot send, modify, or delete anything.
  • Every 30 seconds (configurable) it asks Gmail for new unread messages and checks their sender and subject against your rules.
  • Everything happens on your Mac. MailFlare has no servers; your email never leaves your computer and is never shared with anyone.
  • Quiet hours and a pause control let you decide when alerts are allowed.
Privacy in one sentence: MailFlare reads message headers and previews locally to decide whether to alert you, keeps only a short list of message IDs it has already alerted on, stores your sign-in token in the macOS Keychain, and sends nothing anywhere. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, and a Gmail or Google Workspace account.
